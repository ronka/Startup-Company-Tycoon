# The Hire Roll

Spin for your leadership. A slot-machine reskin of the C-level hiring the game already has.

Status: **plan + prototype**, nothing implemented in `src/game/`.
Prototype: `prototypes/2026-08-14-hire-roll.html` (double-click to open).

> An earlier branch explored a pushed 3-person "founder draft" — see `2026-08-14-founder-draft-mechanic.md`. This supersedes it. That mechanic pushed cohorts at the player on a timer; this one is player-initiated, which is simpler and fits the existing screen.

---

## 1. The mechanic in five lines

1. Open a leadership seat on the Team tab. Press **Spin**.
2. The wheel lands on a **source** — *a friend of your mom*, *someone you studied with*, *ex-Foogle*, or rarely a name you actually know.
3. That source hands you **2 candidates**, always on different perk axes. Hire one, or don't.
4. You get **2 rolls per real day**, shared across all three seats, banking to 6.
5. Better candidates cost more. A bigger company draws from better sources.

Everything else is unchanged. Same three seats, same salary-priced hiring, same `HIRE_CLEVEL`.

## 3. The source ladder — and it already half exists

`clevels.ts` has `EX_EMPLOYERS` keyed by `PerkBand` = `scrappy` / `mid` / `elite`, and `PERK_TABLE` gives each band a non-overlapping `salaryRange`. **That is three of the six tiers, already built and already balance-checked.** This adds two below and one above.

| Tier | Sources | Salary/wk | Perk strength |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Who you can get** | a friend of your mom · your cousin who "does computers" · a guy from your gym · your landlord's nephew | $1.8k–2.8k | 4–10% |
| **Your network** | someone from your last job · someone you studied with · your bootcamp cohort · the intern who got good | $3.2k–5.2k | 10–18% |
| **Scrappy** *(exists)* | ex-Uper · ex-WeWerk · ex-Yahooo · ex-Twitterr | $6k–7k | 16–26% |
| **Solid** *(exists)* | ex-Netflex · ex-Stripee · ex-Airbrb · ex-Snapp | $7.6k–9.5k | 26–38% |
| **Big tech** *(exists)* | ex-Foogle · ex-ClosedAI · ex-Macrosoft · ex-Nvidiaa | $24k–31k | 36–55% |
| **Legend** *(new)* | the person **is** the source — Jensen Wong, Elon Muskk, Sundar Pichaii, Warren Buffet | $52k–78k | 60–95% |

**Salary tracks perk strength inside every band**, and bands never overlap. So "better costs more" is structural, not hand-tuned — and `isDominated()` / `perkPower()` already guard it. Extend the existing property test to the two new bands.

A legend roll hands you **two legends** and one seat. *Jensen Wong or Steve Jobbz* is the best moment in the design.

## 4. The odds

Source probability per roll, by `state.stage` (already on `GameState`). Measured, 40,000 rolls per stage:

| Source tier | Garage | Seed | Series A | Growth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who you can get | 40.0% | 19.7% | 7.8% | 2.0% |
| Your network | 32.3% | 30.3% | 19.9% | 9.9% |
| Scrappy | 19.9% | 28.3% | 27.3% | 20.0% |
| Solid | 6.0% | 14.9% | 25.3% | 28.3% |
| Big tech | 1.4% | 4.9% | 14.9% | 30.0% |
| **Legend** | **0.5%** | **1.9%** | **4.8%** | **9.8%** |

That is the whole "bigger company → better hires" rule: **one lookup on `state.stage`.** No new systems.

### The number that actually matters

Per-stage legend rates are misleading — a median of 136 rolls at Garage reads as "never," but nobody stays in the garage. Simulating a realistic run (16 real days at `WEEKS_PER_DAY = 5` ≈ 80 game-weeks, stages climbing):

| Rolls/day | Rolls/run | Runs that see a legend | Legends/run | Legend lands ~day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **2** | 32 | **81%** | 1.59 | day 11 |
| 3 | 48 | 91% | 2.37 | day 10 |
| 4 | 64 | 96% | 3.17 | day 9 |
| 6 | 96 | 99% | 4.78 | day 8 |

**2/day is the right number and should stay.** Four in five runs get the moment, one in five doesn't — which is what keeps it a moment. At 4/day you get three a run and it stops meaning anything. Every row sees big tech at some point, so no run feels starved.

## 5. The roll budget

Mirrors `week-budget.ts` exactly — same shape, same test style, same `dateKey`:

```ts
export const ROLLS_PER_DAY = 2;
export const ROLLS_BANK_CAP = 6;

export interface RollBudget { lastSessionDate: string; rollsRemaining: number; }

initialRollBudget(now) · refreshRollBudget(budget, now) · canRoll(budget) · spendRoll(budget)
```

Store-layer only. `src/game/` never sees a clock, exactly as `week-budget.ts` documents. New `ROLL_STORAGE_KEY` slice in `game-store.tsx` alongside the week budget and streak.

**One pool shared across three seats**, not two rolls per seat. That makes *"which seat do I spend this on?"* a free, genuine decision — and it's the reason a shared pool beats per-seat.

Banking to 6 means two idle days give a returning player three spins in one sitting. That's the reward for coming back.

## 7. Risks worth deciding on

**A second daily wall.** The game already stops you at `WEEKS_PER_DAY = 5`. This adds a second, independent stop. A player can have 4 game-weeks left and be unable to hire. Mitigations, in order of preference:
- The rolled offer **persists** until you roll again (like `candidates` does today), so running out of rolls never leaves you empty-handed — you keep your last result and can hire from it any time.
- Bank cap 6 smooths short absences.
- Consider granting a roll from the Morning Standup streak, which already varies its reward by `streakDays` and already has a `refreshCandidates` effect to model it on.

**Legends may be unaffordable.** $52k–78k/wk against `STARTING_CASH = 250_000`. That's deliberate — a Growth-stage prize — but check the case where a legend rolls at Seed and simply cannot be hired. A visible "you can't afford this yet" state is better than a hire that bankrupts the run in four weeks.

**Legends land around day 11 of a ~16-day run**, by which point your seats are probably full. So the legend is usually a *replacement*, not a fill. `FIRE_CLEVEL` already exists and costs only `C_LEVEL_DEPARTURE_MORALE_HIT` — confirm that's the right price for swapping in a legend.

**Save shape.** This adds fields to `GameState` and a new persisted slice. The game is live on the App Store and **there is no version gate** — `SAVE_VERSION` was removed pre-release. A shape change silently breaks real players' saves. Decide explicitly: migrate, tolerate, or wipe.

## 9. Build order

1. **Tier data only.** Add `desperate` / `personal` / `legend` to `PERK_TABLE`, `EX_EMPLOYERS`, and a `LEGENDS` pool. Extend the `isDominated` property test. Nothing else changes — the existing 6-candidate offer just gets a wider range.
2. **`roll()` + `STAGE_WEIGHTS`.** Pure, in `src/game/clevels.ts`, fully unit-testable against the odds table in §4.
3. **`RollBudget` slice.** Copy `week-budget.ts`'s shape and its test file.
4. **Wire the picker.** Spin button, source banner, two candidates, the wall.
5. **Animation last.** The spin feel is the easiest part to add and the least risky to defer.

## 10. Open questions

- **Does a bad roll feel funny or annoying?** Only playing tells you. Walkthrough 1 in the prototype is built for exactly this.
- **Should a legend roll offer two legends, or one legend and one ordinary candidate?** Two is more dramatic and a harder choice. Currently two.
- **Re-roll cost.** Rolling again is free (it just spends a roll). Should re-rolling the *same* seat cost cash on top? Probably not — it adds a rule for little gain.
- **Names.** Follow the repo's parody convention (`Linus Torvold`, `ex-Foogle`). No convicted individuals are in the legend pool as written — a deliberate change from the founder-draft branch, and worth keeping.
