## Why these two, and why now

Telemetry from the first 88 installs (PostHog project 505800, 2026-07-10 → 07-25):

| Stage | Players |
|---|---|
| Installed | 88 |
| Started a game | 77 |
| Advanced ≥1 week | 61 |
| **Stopped at exactly week 5** (`WEEKS_PER_DAY`) | **30** |
| Ever got past week 5 | 16 |
| Ever saw an era change (weeks 25–35) | 3 |
| Ever finished a run | 9 |

55 of the 61 players who ever ticked did so on one calendar day. Among players with a
≥3-day window to return, 17% came back for a day 2 (n=75); device-local streak data
agrees (12 of 78 reached streak ≥2). Median first-day engagement span: 3 minutes,
against a designed 5–10.

The shipped depth — eras, 40-card decks, exits, rivals that fight back — sits behind a
gate roughly three-quarters of players never cross, while the players who *do* cross
it reach weeks 10, 14, 15, 16, 24, 45, 50 and 52. The constraint is the day-1 → day-2
bridge, not content depth.

Neither plan changes the size of the daily budget. `plans/prd-endgame-and-depth.md`
§F12 argues that decision deliberately and records the rejected alternative; these
plans make the wall survivable and measurable instead.

## Findings considered and not planned

- **D4 — rebalance weeks 1–5 to carry the pitch.** The run starts empty
  (`STARTING_HEADCOUNT` all zeros, quality 0, customers 0 — `src/game/balance.ts:48`)
  and eras don't begin until week 25 (`balance.ts:734`), so most of the game's depth is
  invisible inside the window 74% of players ever see. Real finding and probably the
  largest available win, but it touches `balance.ts` and risks destabilizing a tuned
  mid-game — it wants a design spike and sim evidence before a build plan, not a
  handoff plan written cold. Revisit after 001/002 are measured; 002's
  `DAY_COMPLETE_SHOWN` breakdown by `agenda_kind` is the instrument that will say how
  badly it's needed.
- **Monetization changes.** Not planned: `paywall_shown` has 7 users,
  `purchase_completed` 2, `purchase_failed` 3. Under-powered — any conclusion drawn
  from it now would be noise.
- **The 2026-07-21 onboarding redesign.** Did not visibly move the funnel
  (install→first-tick 68% → 72%, past-wall 18% → 19%), but n=32 post-redesign. Treat as
  "no signal", not "no effect"; not worth a plan either way yet.
- **`notification_opened` has never fired.** Folded into 001 as a deferred follow-up
  rather than its own plan — 001 first has to make it *possible* for notifications to
  reach anyone (13% of installs currently have permission). If it is still zero once
  the grant rate is healthy, the deep-link handler in `notification-manager.tsx:60-78`
  is the next suspect.
