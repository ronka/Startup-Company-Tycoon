This is how
you do a backflip
First, stand tall
with your feet about
shoulder width apart
Swing your arms back
and bend your knees
Now explode
straight up
and throw your arms
to the sky
At the top
pull your knees
into your chest
The tighter the tuck
the faster you spin
Spot the ground
open up
and land with
soft knees
And that's a backflip
Just maybe try it
on a trampoline first
