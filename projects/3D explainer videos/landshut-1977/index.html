October 1977
Four hijackers take over
a German passenger jet
Lufthansa Flight 181
flying holidaymakers home
from Mallorca
with 91 people on board
For five days
they force it
across the Middle East
demanding the release
of jailed terrorists
In Yemen, they kill the captain
The co-pilot is forced
to fly on
to Mogadishu, Somalia
Then, just after midnight
Somali soldiers light a fire
in front of the cockpit
While the hijackers
stare at the flames
German commandos
climb up to the doors
They burst inside
and storm the cabin
Minutes later
three hijackers are dead
and all 86 passengers
are free
Operation Feuerzauber
Mogadishu
18 October 1977
