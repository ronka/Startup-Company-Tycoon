Hire Roll

A throwaway prototype for Startup Empire Tycoon. Not production code.

The mechanic: open a leadership seat and press Spin. The wheel lands on a source — a friend of your mom, someone from your last job, ex-Foogle, or, rarely, a name you actually know. That source hands you two candidates. Better candidates cost more, and a bigger company draws from better sources.

You get 2 rolls per real day, shared across all three seats, banking to 6. A source can be all-bad — both candidates junk. Your only recourse is another roll, or tomorrow.

The question this answers is not whether spinning feels good. It is whether the stage-gated table produces a legend often enough to chase and rarely enough to matter — and whether 2 rolls a day is generous or stingy when a roll can be wasted.
