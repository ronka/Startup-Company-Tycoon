The Founder Draft

A throwaway prototype for Startup Empire Tycoon. Not production code.

The question this answers: the draft replaces C-level hiring outright. You no longer browse candidates whenever you like — every 12–18 weeks the run pauses and offers you three founders, and you take one. They fill the CTO / CMO / CFO seats.

Each one wants a different mix of cash and equity: a hired gun takes a huge salary and almost no stake; a true believer works nearly free for a big slice of you. So every cohort asks which resource can I afford right now — and the answer flips with your runway.

Two things to judge: does the cash-vs-dilution mix actually change your pick as the run goes on, and at week 50 with all three seats full, is the cohort still a decision? Replacing someone means the equity you already paid them is gone for good.

The weekly simulation is a deliberately crude fake, not the real balance.ts. Read the shape of the choice, not the numbers.
